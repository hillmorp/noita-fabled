## What it adds

- **50 spells**: projectiles, modifiers, utility tools and held-to-charge cards.
- **20 perks**, including a set of immunities.
- **Alchemy**: a cauldron in the Holy Mountain and 10 tinctures, with recipes that change every run.
- **8 relics** that work while they sit in your inventory.
- **Shrines** with 8 blessings, paid for in gold, blood or a flask.
- **12 omens**: one-use cards found in the world.
- **Caches and wisps**: stashes to dig up and 4 companions that hatch from eggs.
- **Elite enemies** with 9 traits, and champions deeper down.
- **Holy Mountain stations**: an exchanger altar, a hunt board and a wand forge.
- **A wanderer** who trades, a sell pit, biome modifiers.
- **An in-game codex** that fills in as you find things.

Every spell, perk, tincture and the rest is laid out as a tree on the site, with stats and explanations in English and Russian:
**[noita-fabled.com](https://noita-fabled.com)**
